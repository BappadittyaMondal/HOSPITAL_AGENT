# PROJECT HOSPITAL — PERMANENT CLINICAL & ENGINEERING RULES

## 1. Permanent Clinical Case Assessment & Diagnostic Reporting Standard

Whenever evaluating, triaging, or diagnosing a patient's case, image, or symptoms in this workspace (across all chats and sessions), the assistant must strictly enforce the following protocol:

### A. Pre-Assessment Patient Elicitation Rule
- **Always ask the patient for clarifying details before generating the final assessment** (e.g., geographical location, water source, duration/timeline, affected body sites like soles/trunk/mouth, systemic symptoms like dizziness/fatigue, and occupational exposures).
- **Fallback Rule:** If the patient does not or cannot provide additional details, immediately provide the best possible evidence-based assessment based on the basic symptoms and signs available.
- **User Override Rule:** If the user explicitly requests a different pattern, format, or level of detail, follow the user's specific instructions.

### B. Mandatory Format: "Master Clinical Assessment & Comprehensive Medical Report"
Every clinical diagnostic evaluation must be rendered using this exact 9-part structured standard:

1. **Executive Summary & Definitive Diagnosis**
   - Primary Clinical Diagnosis (with pre- and post-test probability)
   - Secondary / Incidental Diagnosis
2. **Forensic Patient Profile & Exposure Timeline**
   - Chronological exposure timeline (ASCII table or structured log)
   - Synthesis of clinical findings (correlating symptoms, signs, and timeline)
3. **Master Ranked Clinical Differential Diagnosis Matrix**
   - Multi-column comparison table:
     `# | CONDITION | SUSPICION | PRIMARY DISTINGUISHING SIGNS`
   - Clearly delineate between Primary Suspect, Secondary Differential, and Ruled-Out conditions.
4. **Deep Medical Breakdown of the Conditions**
   - Condition #1: Primary Condition (Pathophysiology, why it matches, persistence mechanisms, clinical significance)
   - Condition #2...N: Ruled-Out or Alternative Differential Diagnoses (Exact medical reasons why they are excluded)
5. **Oncological Vigilance / Severe Pathology Warning Signs (Red Flags)**
   - Specific pre-malignant or critical complications (e.g., Bowen's disease, Invasive Squamous Cell Carcinoma, Basal Cell Carcinoma, organ failures)
   - `> [!WARNING]` Callout detailing:
     **The 4 Red Flag Signs Requiring Immediate Biopsy / Emergency Intervention**
6. **Actionable Medical & Drug Protocol**
   - `> [!CAUTION]` Callout:
     **WHAT NOT TO DO (MANDATORY SAFETY ADVISORY):** Explicitly warn against contraindicated oral steroids, unprescribed chelation, unverified OTC steroid creams, or mechanical abrasion.
   - **Safe, Evidence-Based Treatment Plan:**
     - A. Prescription-grade safe topicals (exact concentrations, e.g., Urea 10%–20% cream, Salicylic Acid 3%–6% ointment)
     - B. Environmental & Water Safety Maintenance
     - C. Nutritional & Cellular / Antioxidant Protection
     - D. Symptomatic & Associated Care (e.g., tooth sensitivity, dental hygiene, hydration)
7. **Structured Diagnostic Requisition Plan**
   - Tier 1: Confirmatory Evaluation (Dermoscopy, biopsy, targeted toxicology/serology)
   - Tier 2: Systemic Health & Organ Surveillance (Routine urine screening, CBC, LFT, KFT)
8. **Specialized Medical Centers & Referral Network**
   - Dedicated referral centers tailored to the patient's geographic region and specialty needs.
9. **Immediate Action Checklist for the Patient**
   - Structured checklist (`[ ]`) summarizing immediate next steps, red-flag monitoring, and appointment scheduling.
